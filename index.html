About

From roll-rates to retention curves

At HSBC and Wells Fargo, Aoshi spent seven years building the instruments banks use to decide who gets credit and how much: risk-tier models, swap-set experiments, forecasting platforms that stitch years of behavioral data into a single trustworthy number. The math underneath — hypothesis testing, causal inference, time-series forecasting — is the same math product teams use to decide what to build next.

This site tracks that recalibration in the open: real projects, run on public product data, using the same rigor she's applied to credit portfolios for years. Nothing here is a finished museum piece — it's a working log of a transition in progress.
